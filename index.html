DATA

Research group at Département d'Informatique of the École Normale Supérieure.

We develop foundational mathematical and algorithmic approaches for high-dimensional learning and generative modeling. Our research focuses on representation learning, statistical inference, privacy, and generative models with applications across physics, audio, vision, and neuroscience.

We are hosted at the interdisciplinary ENS Center for AI, the Centre de Sciences des Données (CSD). Members of the team are also actively involved in the PR[AI]RIE - PSAI (Paris School of AI) Cluster. DATA is a joint research team between the École Normale Supérieure and the CNRS.

The DATA group also hosts the MathAData and the Challenge Data initiatives detailed in the Projects section.
